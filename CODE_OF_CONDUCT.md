# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a respectful, inclusive, and evidence-focused community. Questions
about basic R usage or statistical interpretation are welcome.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Separate statistical disagreement from personal judgment.
- Protect confidential research data.
- Disclose uncertainty, assumptions, and limitations.
- Credit contributors and underlying methods appropriately.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation of
results, and publication of private data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. The maintainer
may edit or remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate
action. For private reports and enforcement questions, follow the contact process
described in `SECURITY.md`.
